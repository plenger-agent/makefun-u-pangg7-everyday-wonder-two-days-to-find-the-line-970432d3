## The sentence

> The streetlight flickered on above me, and for one block I walked inside my own spotlight.

(90 characters, one sentence.)

A streetlight switching on is the most ordinary thing on an evening walk — but for one block, the light makes the walker the lit subject of the street, as if the night had put a spotlight on them. The surprise lands in the last words, and nothing in it is explained.
